## About

**I am currently searching for PhD students. Please get in touch via my email above if you have strong qualifications and are interested in optimisation for deep learning.**

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Bath. 
My research focuses on the **Foundations of Deep Learning**. Deep learning is everywhere, yet most of it is poorly understood and based on heuristics. I want to improve the core algorithms it relies upon, whether that involves optimisation, efficiency, uncertainty quantification, theory, or anything else!

I did my PhD at the University of Bristol from 2021-2025, supervised by Dr Laurence Aitchison.
